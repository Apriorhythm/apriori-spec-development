# RUNBOOK delta 草案——双语可评审文本（设计阶段；实现 change 合入 RUNBOOK.md / RUNBOOK_cn.md 时以此为准逐句对应）

## Section: The hotfix lane (minimal write-back unit outside the process) / 新节一：Hotfix 通道（流程边界外的最小回写单元）

**EN:**
> **The hotfix lane** is the thinnest compliant path for three kinds of after-the-fact write-back: (1) post-hoc records of urgent code fixes; (2) investigation conclusions where the verdict is "no fix needed"; (3) business facts learned after go-live (KB Decisions append). It is **not a seventh step of the process** — it lives outside STEP0-6, and the five proven protections of the never-weaken list are untouched inside the formal process.
>
> **Admission is mechanical, by blast radius.** The grading function (fields contract + cross-field invariants + ordered decision table; see the tables in this section) outputs `(radius, R2-subtype)`. `R3` — REMOVED/RENAMED deltas, cross-module unions ≥ 2, dual-end touches, scenario-less blocks, out-of-bound Decisions, and any MODIFIED/ADDED block that hits **no demotion whitelist** — is **rejected with a pointer to the formal process** (fail-up is the default). The demotion whitelists are: a human-owned `blast: low` annotation on the store side; and, **only if ruling Q-12=yes**, the ADDED-only machine whitelist (single-module, every block carries a scenario).
>
> **Archiving goes through the dedicated `apriori hotfix` subcommand.** The ordinary `archive --change` and its zero-delta fail-closed guard (AM-17) are untouched.
>
> **Honest boundaries.** Declared fields are the machine's input; their truthfulness cannot be mechanically verified — outright concealment is caught by the compensation chain (mandatory conclusion, auditable archives, visibility to later formal reviews), not by the grader. `no-test` debt (if retained by ruling) always has an archived carrier; **the zero-delta case is special in having no living-store projection** — for keyed no-test on a real delta, the archived-gate blocking consequence is **conditional** on the scenario actually having no valid non-skip TAP result. External-repo fix references are handled per the ruled w/x options (w2: honestly marked `unverifiable`; w1: rejected). Unannotated single-file docs fixes grade R3 — stricter than today's docs trivial; that tightening is an explicit owner decision.

**CN:**
> **Hotfix 通道**是三类事后回写的最薄合规路径：（1）紧急代码修复的事后补记；（2）"不用修"的排查结论；（3）上线后学到的业务事实（KB Decisions 追加）。它**不是流程的第七步**——它在 STEP0-6 之外，别改清单五项在正式流程内分毫不动。
>
> **准入按爆炸半径机械判定。**分级函数（字段契约+跨字段不变量+判定次序表；表随本节收录）输出 `(半径, R2 子型)`。`R3`——REMOVED/RENAMED、跨模块并集 ≥2、双端触及、无 scenario 块、越界 Decisions、以及未命中**任何降级白名单**的 MODIFIED/ADDED 块（fail-up 为默认）——**拒绝并指路正式流程**。白名单两条：store 侧人类授予的 `blast: low` 标注；以及**仅当裁定 Q-12=yes** 的 ADDED-only 机械白名单（单模块、每块含 scenario）。
>
> **归档走专属 `apriori hotfix` 子命令。**普通 `archive --change` 及其 zero-delta fail-closed 护栏（AM-17）分毫不动。
>
> **诚实边界。**申报字段是机器的输入，真实性不可机械验证——整体瞒报由补偿链（结论强制、归档可审计、后续正式评审可见）承接，不由分级器承接。`no-test` 债务（若裁定保留）任何案均有归档载体；**零 delta 案的特殊点是无 living-store 投影**——非零 delta 的 keyed no-test，archived gate 阻塞后果**条件式**成立（该场景实际无有效非 skip TAP 结果时）。外仓修复引用按 w/x 裁定案处理（w2 案：如实标注 `unverifiable`；w1 案：拒绝）。未标注的单文件 docs 修复判 R3——比现行 docs trivial 更严；该收紧是显式的 owner 决定。

## Section: Mechanical scaling of verification strength / 新节二：验证强度的机械化缩放

**EN:**
> Declare **`verification-profile`** in `process-config.md` (human-owned, agent read-only; absent = today's honor-system, no escalation). Once declared, the profile's evidence requirements become **tier requirements**: machine-judged results (`verify` GREEN, E2E artifacts) are mechanical exit conditions at every tier; human-verifiable artifacts and explicit waivers are **mandatory at the full tier and advisory at the incremental tier**. (In this increment the mechanical enforcement is implemented for the hotfix lane only. Whether the formal-flow side is mechanised here or by a follow-up change is **an open owner decision**; until it is ruled, the formal-flow wording below is a **process requirement**, not a mechanical block.)
>
> **Three evidence classes.** (a) Machine-judged results — `verify` GREEN and E2E artifacts — must **exist and PASS** (a FAIL artifact never satisfies an exit condition). (b) Human-verifiable artifacts — screenshot observation records (path, one-line observation, time, code baseline, run id) — must exist **at the full tier** (advisory at the incremental tier); the image's retention follows the ruled π option — π1: instrument under `apriori/tmp/`, verifiable at presentation time; π2 (contract deliberately incomplete in this package — see design D7; choosing it requires a follow-up design increment before implementation): copied into the bundle at `evidence/screenshots/<basename>` by the author-side `apriori hotfix evidence` command — the `src=` path must live under `apriori/tmp/`, so an image kept outside the repo must first be moved there by hand (that step counts as one more command against the cost budget); records carry `src=` before the copy, `path=` after; π3: kept in place at a repo-relative path with a recorded hash, containment-checked and read through a checked fd. (c) Explicit waivers — `ui: not-applicable — <reason>` — must exist with a non-empty reason **at the full tier**; optional at the incremental tier but validated when present.
>
> **What is mandatory is evidence existence; what scales is evidence coverage.** Full tier (medium/large): the current matrix, plus — under a `ui` profile — the full E2E suite and screenshot evidence (**a process requirement from this change on; whether a mechanical exit condition backs it is pending the owner's scope ruling**). Incremental tier — **hotfix**: test proof over the affected scope only, per the ruled Q-3 option (i: in-place scoped verify; ii: referenced evidence contract) and the ruled Q-4 no-test option where applicable; screenshots are **advisory at this tier** — when frontend files are touched and no record line is present the run prints a reminder but does not block; a record line, once written, is validated in full. The mandatory screenshot record and the explicit `not-applicable` line apply to the **full tier only**. **Formal trivial**: keeps state-A tests + verify GREEN unconditionally (Q-3/Q-4 do not apply — only the *coverage* may be the affected set). Backend-only changes under a `ui` profile leave an explicit `not-applicable` line **at the full tier** (optional at the incremental tier). The formal process **never inherits** hotfix waivers: formal trivial always keeps state-A tests + verify GREEN.

**CN:**
> 在 `process-config.md` 声明 **`verification-profile`**（human-owned、agent 只读；缺省=现状自觉制、不升格）。声明后，该 profile 的证据要求成为对应 tier 的**要求**：机械判结论者（`verify` GREEN、E2E 工件）在各档均为机械退出条件；人类可查证物与显式豁免物的存在性**全量档强制、增量档 advisory**。（本增量的机械强制只落在 hotfix 通道。正式流程侧的机械化在本轮做还是留后续 change，**尚待 owner 裁定**；裁定前下文对正式流程的表述是**流程要求**，不是机械阻断。）
>
> **证据三层。**（a）机械判结论——`verify` GREEN 与 E2E 工件——必须**存在且 PASS**（FAIL 工件永不满足退出条件）。（b）人类可查证物——截图观察记录（路径、一行观察、时间、代码基线、运行标识）——**全量档必须存在**（增量档 advisory）；图片留存按 π 裁定案——π1：`apriori/tmp/` 仪器、呈阅时点可查证；π2（本包内契约有意未完成——见 design D7；裁 π2 须先补后续设计增量方可实现）：由作者侧 `apriori hotfix evidence` 命令复制入 bundle 的 `evidence/screenshots/<basename>`——`src=` 必须位于 `apriori/tmp/` 下，仓外图片须先手工搬入（该动作按一条命令计入成本预算）；记录行复制前携 `src=`、复制后为 `path=`；π3：留在仓根相对路径原处并记录哈希，经 containment 检查、以受检 fd 读取。（c）显式豁免物——`ui: not-applicable — <理由>`——**全量档**必须存在且理由非空；增量档可选，但出现即校验。
>
> **强制的是证据存在性，缩放的是证据覆盖面。**全量档（medium/large）：现行矩阵，且 `ui` profile 下全量 E2E 与截图证据（**自本 change 起为流程要求；是否有机械退出条件背书，待 owner 的范围裁定**）。增量档——**hotfix**：只对受影响 scope 做测试证明，按 Q-3 裁定案（i 现场 scoped verify / ii 证据引用契约）与适用处的 Q-4 no-test 裁定案；该档的**截图为 advisory**——touch 前端文件而无记录行时打印提示、不阻塞归档；一旦写了记录行则全谱校验。强制的截图记录与显式 `not-applicable` 行**只适用于全量档**。**正式 trivial**：无条件保持 state A 的 tests + verify GREEN（Q-3/Q-4 不适用——仅测试**覆盖面**可取受影响集）。`ui` profile 下的纯后端 change **在全量档**留显式 `not-applicable` 行（增量档可选）。正式流程**永不继承** hotfix 豁免：正式 trivial 恒保持 state A 的 tests + verify GREEN。

## Phrase-table delta（r5 DES5-8——canonical 短语与尾注 grammar，EN/CN 同文）

**EN:** The review phrase table gains the hotfix-lane verdict grammar: a verdict line is `VERDICT: <phrase> role=<r> digest=<hex> [boundary=<b>]` — **role and digest are mandatory, exactly once each (missing/duplicate = reject); **digest is exactly 64 lowercase hex chars**; only boundary is conditionally optional** where `<r>` ∈ {inspection, p8}, `<b>` ∈ {within, exceeds}; legal phrase pairs — role=inspection → `no findings` | `<N> issues open` (N a positive decimal integer); role=p8 → `no spec-vs-code gaps` | `gaps found`. **`boundary` requiredness is two-way: it is mandatory on the inspection line when a γ'-scoped inspection substitutes for sign-off d (missing = reject), and illegal anywhere else (present = reject) — `p8+boundary` and plain-inspection+boundary are invalid lines.** Existing state-A phrases are untouched; the trailing annotations are extensions the C5 parser tolerates (prefix `VERDICT:` preserved) and the hotfix preflight consumes.

**CN:** 评审 phrase table 增补 hotfix 通道 verdict grammar：verdict 行 = `VERDICT: <短语> role=<r> digest=<hex> [boundary=<b>]`——**role 与 digest 必填且各恰一（缺失/重复 = 拒）；**digest 恰 64 位小写 hex**；仅 boundary 条件选填**，`<r>` ∈ {inspection, p8}、`<b>` ∈ {within, exceeds}；合法短语配对——role=inspection → `no findings` | `<N> issues open`（N 为十进制正整数）；role=p8 → `no spec-vs-code gaps` | `gaps found`。**boundary 的 requiredness 双向：γ' 点检替代签收 d 时 inspection 行必填（缺 = 拒）；其余任何行出现即非法（`p8+boundary`、普通 inspection+boundary 均拒）**。state A 既有短语不动；尾注为扩展（`VERDICT:` 前缀保持、C5 兼容），由 hotfix preflight 消费。

## Platform note（r17 DES17-3——EN/CN 同文）

**EN:** The f2 freshness option hashes evidence through a safe open (`O_NOFOLLOW` + `fstat` regular + dev/inode match), performed per file on its real path. It applies whenever **f2, a π2 copy, π3, or any `hash=` field** is in play. Where the constant is missing or the platform/filesystem does not support it, that step is **fail-closed with no fallback** — hotfix archiving is unavailable **for bundles that trigger it**. A combination that triggers none of the four conditions (e.g. π1 with {f1} and no `hash=`) archives normally on the same platform. A platform that silently ignores the flag cannot be detected (declared residual). Projects targeting such platforms must avoid **all four** triggers — i.e. rule a non-f2 freshness set, avoid π2 copies and π3, and omit optional `hash=` fields.

**CN:** f2 新鲜度案以安全打开（`O_NOFOLLOW` + `fstat` regular + dev/inode 一致）逐文件在真实路径上散列证据。其适用面为 **f2 ∨ π2 复制 ∨ π3 ∨ 任一 `hash=` 出现**；常量缺失或平台/文件系统不支持时，该步骤**fail-closed 且无 fallback**——该平台上**触发该条件的 bundle** 无法归档；未触发四条件中任一者的组合（如 π1 + {f1} + 无 `hash=`）在同一平台照常可归档。静默忽略该 flag 的平台不可检出（声明级残余）。目标环境含此类平台的项目须避开**全部四个触发条件**——即改裁非 f2 的新鲜度集合，且不选 π2 复制与 π3、不填可选 `hash=`。
