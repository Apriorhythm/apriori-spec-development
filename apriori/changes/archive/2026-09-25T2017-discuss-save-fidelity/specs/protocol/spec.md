# Delta — protocol (discuss-save-fidelity)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: a discuss save is a faithful record of the discussion, nothing more
The discuss stance (runbook §4 "Discuss first", the P6 prompt, and the `/apriori-discuss` shell shipped as `templates/discuss.md`) SHALL state, in both language editions, that a save writes exactly what the discussion produced, in four kinds and no others: `decision` in the human's words (a faithful paraphrase allowed; reasons, conditions or risk acceptances the human did not state are not), `observed` facts actually read with their source, `assumption` labelled as such, and `## Open` for the questions the discussion left open. It SHALL state that a save never adds a commitment, a risk acceptance, a requirement or a blocking item that was not raised in the discussion; never promotes an assumption or the agent's own recommendation into a decision or an observed fact; keeps a question the human explicitly left open open; and that saving again with nothing new changes nothing of substance (no duplicated lines, no new items, existing facts and progress kept) and never starts development. The shell SHALL remain a thin pointer (it does not copy the runbook paragraph verbatim), its golden and the discuss generation table SHALL follow the new body, and the behavioural acceptance of the rule (real-client runs) is recorded outside this repository. Evidence for the rule: FC3Y adjudication — a save that attached a risk-acceptance rationale the owner never gave, and a save that added an undiscussed blocking item.

#### Scenario: DS-14 the discuss shell carries the save-fidelity rule and stays thin
- WHEN `templates/discuss.md` is read
- THEN it says, in one compact sentence that points at §4 "Discuss first" for the full rule, that a save is a faithful record: the four kinds with `decision` in their words, nothing the discussion did not raise (no added reason, risk acceptance, requirement or blocker), no assumption or advice promoted, what they left open stays open, re-saving with nothing new changes nothing of substance; the shell stays under its thin-shell size bound and still does not contain the runbook's paragraph verbatim; the golden equals the template; the discuss generation table's newest entry is the template's digest

#### Scenario: DS-15 both runbook editions state the rule in §4 and mirror it in P6
- WHEN `RUNBOOK.md` and `RUNBOOK_cn.md` are read
- THEN each carries the faithful-record paragraph in §4 "Discuss first", every safeguard pinned on its own — the four kinds, `decision` in the human's words with unstated reasons / conditions / risk acceptances excluded, no added commitment / risk acceptance / requirement / blocking item, no promotion, open stays open and is not decided for them, re-save changes nothing of substance, never starts development — and the P6 prompt block contains a mirror in the human's voice that names each of: my words only, no added reasons / conditions / risk acceptances / requirements / blocking items, assumption stays assumption, recommendation is not my decision, left open stays open, re-save changes nothing of substance; deleting any one safeguard fails its own assertion
