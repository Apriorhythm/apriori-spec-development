# Delta — protocol (prototype-walk-guide)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: the runbook offers a prototype walk at Ground and sends the agent to the guide before it runs one
§4 Ground SHALL say: when a requirement's sources include a runnable UI prototype meant as an acceptance basis and nothing on record walks it for this scope (a `checklist.md` registered from a walk of the same prototype version), the agent offers the owner an optional prototype walk — once per requirement, when its sources are first registered — and records the answer as a `decision` so later changes see it; an authorized walk is run, a choice already made is not asked again, and screenshots or a static design are not a runnable prototype. Before running a walk the agent reads `apriori/guides/prototype-walk.md` in full and follows it. The walk's checklist is source material; the prototype's own defects follow the requirement sources and recorded decisions first, and only what nothing settles goes to the owner as an `## Open` item; skipping the walk skips none of P2's source checks. The runbook's header SHALL no longer claim to hold everything at runtime: it names the one guide that lives beside it and when it is read. No new stop and no machine check.

#### Scenario: PW-04 Ground carries the offer, the once-per-requirement rule and the read-before-running pointer, and the header names the guide
- WHEN §4 Ground and the runbook's header are read
- THEN Ground says when to offer a walk (a runnable prototype meant as acceptance basis, no walk on record for this scope and version), once per requirement with the answer recorded as a `decision`, that an authorized walk runs and a made choice is not asked again, that screenshots or a static design do not qualify, that the agent reads `apriori/guides/prototype-walk.md` in full before running a walk, that its checklist is source material and prototype defects follow the sources and recorded decisions before reaching the owner, and that skipping it skips none of P2's source checks; the header says the runbook is self-contained but for that one guide, read only when a walk runs; and P3 is byte-identical to its frozen form (RIB-10)

### Requirement: the guide states its entry, its checklist and its hard constraints, and the operator guide tells the human how to ask for a walk
`guides/prototype-walk.md` (English) SHALL open with a parameterized entry (prototype and its version, sources and their versions, scope, caps set before running, where the kept artifacts and the scratch go) and a one-sentence form a human can paste; SHALL generalize the walk's method (static analysis, a local server, runtime discovery, a state model, signatures with a stated equivalence basis, traversal, interaction and boundary coverage, path-explosion control by component class, errors, screenshots, coverage with uncovered/blocked/duplicate/injected-only paths) without naming one requirement's components; SHALL define the reconciliation checklist with its minimal fields (`ID`, `version`, `observable`, `evidence`, `scope`, `ruling`, `implementation`, `verification`) as the one progress source — `scope` saying whether the delivery must show the row (`in` / `out`) apart from `ruling` saying how (`as prototype`, `corrected — <source>`, `not built — <reason>`, `owner — <Open id>`), so a prototype defect the sources correct is an `in` row reconciled against its ruling; a screenshot a row cites kept under the archived `OUT/evidence/`; and say that a later check ticks every `in` row against the implementation while checking the assertion and run conditions behind each tick, and that a later change reuses a walk for the scope it covered at that prototype version and, with the owner's authorization, continues it for what it left uncovered, blocked or out of reach — in place while the checklist's change is in flight, and once that change is archived in a successor checklist in the current change that carries every row with its id and names its archived predecessor, which is never edited (the same for a later reconciliation filling `implementation` and `verification`); SHALL state four hard constraints (equivalence justified and injected states kept apart; caps set before running and never a coverage claim; tools optional, not apriori-cli dependencies, the prototype unmodified; isolation and scrubbing of everything written); and SHALL say how the result is registered in Ground. `docs/operator.md` and `docs/operator_cn.md` SHALL each carry a short paragraph telling the human when the agent offers a walk, how to ask for one, what it needs on the machine, and that its checklist authorizes nothing.

#### Scenario: PW-05 the guide carries its entry, method, checklist fields and four constraints, and both operator editions point a human at it
- WHEN `guides/prototype-walk.md`, `docs/operator.md` and `docs/operator_cn.md` are read
- THEN the guide has the entry table with its parameters and the paste-able sentence, the method sections, the checklist with the eight fields (scope apart from ruling; corrected defects are `in` rows; cited screenshots kept under `OUT/evidence/`; reuse for the covered scope and authorized continuation otherwise — in place while the change is in flight, in a successor checklist once it is archived, never editing the frozen one), the four hard constraints, the completion criteria (uncovered work is reported, never "all done") and the Ground registration line, and names none of one requirement's components as its own rules; each operator edition has the prototype-walk paragraph naming the guide, the tools it needs and that the checklist authorizes nothing

### Requirement: MIGRATING states what the guide changes for a project and that downgrading is not supported
MIGRATING.md SHALL say that `apriori update` installs the guide on a project that never had it, that a locally edited guide is left alone like the runbook, and that a CLI from before this change refuses a manifest listing the guide (`not a refresh target`), so downgrading the CLI is not supported once the guide is recorded.

#### Scenario: PW-06 MIGRATING names the first install and the downgrade limit
- WHEN MIGRATING.md's prototype-walk-guide section is read
- THEN it names `apriori/guides/prototype-walk.md`, the first install by `apriori update`, the edited-guide protection, and the older CLI's `not a refresh target` refusal as a downgrade limit

## Notes

Why: the owner decided on 2026-10-02 that the prototype-walk specification used on a real requirement enters apriori as a separate guide the runbook references, not as more runbook text, and that the runbook is not split now (Claude × Astra plan C-b, R59-CONSENSUS §四).
