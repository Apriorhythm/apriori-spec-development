## Newly found substantive issues

- **AU-6 — The headline state is computed from attributable rounds only, so a LATER round that exists on disk but is unattributable or erased is demoted to a waived note while the state says the review converged.** `rounds` and `unresolved` are built from `factsSeen.families` / `loopSeen.families` ([readiness.js:394–404](/root/terra/asd-v62-code/lib/readiness.js)), and `validateEvidence` admits a round to a family only when its summary classifies AND has a raw (or legal provenance). Every doc that fails that test — a later revise with no raw, a later summary whose VERDICT line was removed beside its transcript, an orphan later transcript — becomes a `facts.problems` / `missingRaw` entry, which the closeout moves into `owed` and waives. Reproduced with `readinessOf` on crafted bundles: (a) attributable `code-review-v1.md` accept + `code-review-v2.md` `2 issues open` with no raw → `evidence incomplete — code-review round 1 (accept)`, the round-2 revise only in the waived note; (b) accept round 1 + round 2 with its raw present but its VERDICT line deleted → `evidence incomplete — code-review round 1 (accept)` with the waived note itself reading *"a round's verdict cannot be removed to lower the round"* — the closeout then lowers it exactly that way; (c) accept round 1 + an orphan `code-review-v2-raw.txt` → same. The contract promises the state "SHALL be read from the families' own last verdicts" and defines `evidence incomplete` as "every family converged"; a family whose latest claim on disk is a revise, or an erased round, has not converged in any sense the owner's requirement (「评审未收敛」及最后一轮结论) means. AM-139's mixed-attribution case covers only the benign ordering (unattributable round 1, attributable accepting round 2). **Risk:** the durable declaration and manifest state the review converged for a change whose last review on disk said otherwise — the one thing this feature exists to say truthfully. **Fix:** classify a family as `not converged` whenever the evidence names an ordinal beyond its last attributable round (the `present` set vs `rounds` in `validateEvidence` already knows this; expose it, e.g. a per-family `claimedBeyond` or the max claimed ordinal) or when any of its docs carries a `verdict-deleted` / `orphan-round-raw` / `unclassified` / `conflicting-verdict` finding; name that doc in the `rounds` text as `(unattributable)`; add the reverse-order mixed case and the verdict-deleted case to AM-139 and assert `not converged`.

- **AU-7 — The closeout waives every evidence-integrity finding, which the contract lists in neither of its two relaxed demands and the code's own comments call structural.** `for (const p of loop.problems) owed.push(…)` ([readiness.js:732](/root/terra/asd-v62-code/lib/readiness.js)) carries all of `facts.problems` — `ordinal-gap` ("integrity, not tidiness… a missing round cannot be deleted away"), `duplicate-ordinal`, `verdict-deleted`, `orphan-round-raw`, `conflicting-verdict`, `unclassified`, `provenance-verdict-missing` — into the waivable set. Reproduced: accept round 1 + accept round 3 with round 2 deleted → `ready=true`, `evidence incomplete — code-review round 3 (accept)`, the gap a waived note; accept round 1 + a round-2 doc declaring two different outcomes → `ready=true`, `evidence incomplete`. The ADDED requirement enumerates what R4 waives (no round, revising/escalated, missing or failing `review-progress`, verdict doc with no raw) and what still refuses (root and file-type guards); these anti-tamper findings are in neither list, so the contract is silent and the code chose the permissive side — against the owner's 「不放行…结构缺陷」. The normal path refuses all of them non-forceably. **Risk:** a bundle whose review history has been edited (a round removed, a verdict contradicted or made non-machine-readable) closes with a converged-class state and a frozen manifest built from what remains. **Fix:** keep `missingRaw`, `loop.progress`, `reviewFloor`, escalations and stops as the waivable loop obligations; route `facts.problems` of the integrity kinds into `blockers` as `class: 'structural'` under the closeout (one line next to the `unreadable-doc` handling at [readiness.js:376](/root/terra/asd-v62-code/lib/readiness.js)); say so in the contract's structural-guard list and in RUNBOOK §4 / docs; add an ordinal-gap and a conflicting-verdict counterexample to AM-140. (If the owner instead wants these waived, the contract must say so and AU-6's classification must still report them as `not converged`.)

- **AU-8 — The two MODIFIED requirement sentences still describe a two-state closeout.** The "three states" block says the critical-evidence state "SHALL also say `independent review: none` or `not converged`", and the manifest block says `"independentReview": "none" | "not converged"` ([specs/archive-merge/spec.md](/root/terra/asd-v62-code/apriori/changes/archive-unreviewed/specs/archive-merge/spec.md), MODIFIED sections), while the ADDED requirement, the code, AM-139/AM-141, CHANGELOG, RUNBOOK §4 and both CLI docs carry four states. These sentences are what `archive` merges into the store as the standing contract; merged as they stand, the store contradicts itself and the code. **Risk:** spec-vs-code gap in the exact text being published; a later reader of the store cannot tell whether `evidence incomplete` / `converged` are legal manifest values. **Fix:** update both sentences to the four states (one phrase each); nothing else in the blocks needs to move — I verified all three MODIFIED blocks keep every store scenario (AM-74…AM-114, AM-118/119, AM-128…AM-131) byte-for-byte.

## Risk surfaces examined

## Advisories

- The converged-at-`review` refusal reads `the independent review converged — there is nothing to close without it` even when the family actually escalated and was answered by `accept-risk` + `--force` (the forced entry leaves `owed` empty). The refusal is right — the normal path with `--force` is the exit — but the sentence is false; say "nothing left for the closeout to waive" instead.
- A reason given only on a continuation line (`… archive-unreviewed c —` then `    delivered` on the next line) is a grant, and the recorded `decision` is the reason-less first line. Consistent with `dropGrant`/`forceGrant`, so not a defect of this change; worth a note in the docs or recording `joined` instead.
- A subdirectory or non-`.md` symlink under `review/` is refused by the closeout but silently skipped by `readReviewDir` on the normal path; the message `review/<n> is not a regular file` names no cure. No real bundle here has one.
- AM-140's WHEN/THEN claims the drop guard and conflicts refuse under the closeout, and the ADDED requirement names `done`; none of the three is in the test. Either add them (one case each) or trim the scenario text to what it asserts.
- `doctor` D7 will report a build-phase closeout as `archived … @ build — archived before review; frozen as is`, which for the converged-at-build case reads oddly beside the manifest's `converged`. Information only.
- Minor: `check` prints `(unknown)` when a `review` object lacks `independentReview`; fine as a fail-safe, but nothing writes such a manifest.
