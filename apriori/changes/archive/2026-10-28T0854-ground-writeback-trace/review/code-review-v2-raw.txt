## Advisories (do not count toward the verdict)

- **A-5 — "builds or replaces" vs. modifies.** The routing sentence in Ground and P1 covers a
  write-back the change "builds or replaces". The common case is that the change *extends* the
  existing adapter or serializer, and it is covered only implicitly, through the Specify
  runtime-update clause. "builds, replaces or changes the write-back" would make the routing
  explicit. Wording only.
- **A-6 — the docs no-mechanism scan reads to end of file.** The `added()` helper slices from
  the fragment to the end of the file. For `docs/concepts.md` that is about 127 lines, and for
  `docs/cli.md` about 86, of unrelated handbook text. Those lines are tested against
  `/diagram|a new document|…/i` and `/…|Plane/i`. A future, unrelated docs edit, such as a
  section mentioning a diagram or a control plane, would turn PR-76 red for no reason. The CN
  editions are not scanned. Suggest slicing to the end of the line or paragraph.
- **A-7 — "new code" vs. "changed code".** The spec Requirement's parenthetical still says "a
  saved document whose contents the **new** code will read…", while RUNBOOK.md:290 now says
  "the **changed** code". The meaning is the same and no assertion depends on it. Align for
  consistency.
- **A-3** (carried, not addressed by the producer's choice): see above.
