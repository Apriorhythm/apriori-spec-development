# Delta — protocol (preserve-source-outcomes)

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: a source's outcome is not reduced when it becomes an expected result, nor when a review suggests a different result
The runbook SHALL say, in §4 Specify's "Minimal means minimal" rule, that for the examples carrying a conflict Ground observed between a source and current behavior the producer returns to the source itself: cites the applicable clause and states each required observable result separately, preserving its conditions and alternatives; that equivalent wording is sufficient but a consequence that can also hold when a required result is absent is not; and that the expected result excludes the conflicting outcome observed in current behavior. This sentence SHALL replace the PR-78 tail "each expected result retains every source obligation applicable to that example". The runbook SHALL say, in §4 Review & Deliver's disposition rule, that before accepting a suggested fix that changes an observable result stated by a source the producer cites the applicable source clause and states the difference in the finding's disposition; that current behavior, convention or a review recommendation alone does not authorize that change and the contract is not rewritten to make it a fix; that the source's result is retained unless a source or a recorded owner decision authorizes the difference; and that an independently supported applicable constraint preventing that is recorded with both bases and left pending the owner under the existing rules, never as a non-blocking follow-up. P2's scenario bullet and P2's Build & Test paragraph SHALL carry the same two instructions to the producer. P1 and P3 SHALL read exactly as before.

#### Scenario: PR-79 the runbook returns expected results to the source and compares a review-advised change with the source before accepting it
- WHEN §4 Specify's "Minimal means minimal" rule, §4 Review & Deliver's disposition rule, P1, P2 and P3 are read
- THEN the Specify rule says that for the examples carrying an observed source-vs-current conflict the producer returns to the source itself, cites the applicable clause, states each required observable result separately preserving its conditions and alternatives, accepts equivalent wording but not a consequence that can also hold when a required result is absent, and excludes the conflicting outcome observed in current behavior; the PR-78 tail "retains every source obligation applicable to that example" no longer appears, while PR-78's entry-point coverage and PR-75/PR-77 text stay
- AND the disposition rule says that before accepting a suggested fix that changes an observable result stated by a source the producer cites the source clause and states the difference, that current behavior, convention or a review recommendation alone does not authorize the change, that the contract is not rewritten to make it a fix, that the source's result is retained unless a source or a recorded owner decision authorizes the difference, and that an independently supported applicable constraint preventing that is recorded with both bases and left pending the owner, not as a non-blocking follow-up; the owner-decision sentence and the PR-75 entry-point sentence stay
- AND P2's scenario bullet says, for each such example, to cite the applicable source clause and separately state all observable results it requires under that condition preserving its alternatives, equivalent wording sufficing but not a consequence that also permits the observed conflicting outcome; and P2's Build & Test paragraph ends by saying that when responding to review a proposed change to a source-stated result is compared with the source itself, its difference and authority recorded in the disposition, the contract not rewritten merely to accept the recommendation, and a genuine unresolved constraint conflict left pending the owner
